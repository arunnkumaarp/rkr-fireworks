Do I pay online?

No. You send an estimate here, we call you to confirm the total, and then you pay by UPI or bank transfer.

How is it delivered?

By lorry transport to the transport office nearest you. We share the LR number so you can track and collect it.

Who pays for transport?

You pay the transport charges at the office when you collect your parcel.

Can I change my order after sending it?

Yes. Tell us on the confirmation call and we update the estimate before you pay.

Where do you deliver?

Only where the sale of firecrackers is allowed. Tell us your town on the call and we will confirm.

What if something arrives damaged?

Send us photos on WhatsApp within 24 hours of collecting and we will sort it out.
